Make room for thought.

Simple Note is a small, fast notebook for your Mac. One window, notes sorted by when you wrote them, saved on your Mac as you type.

This is a working preview. Pick a note, search, or type in it.

Made for the Mac

A native app with the standard Mac sidebar, search and find bar you already know, in light or dark.

Your notes stay on your Mac

Everything saves as you type to a single file on your Mac. Nothing to sync, no account to make before you can write.

Write first, format later

Use *asterisks* for italic and **double** for bold. Press ⌘↩ to see it rendered, then press it again to keep writing.

A good notebook doesn’t ask what you’re making. It simply makes space for it.
