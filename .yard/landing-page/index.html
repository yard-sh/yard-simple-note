There’s a particular kind of light in early spring — low and slow, angled across the floor like it’s considering whether to stay.

01 Principles

Three things. Nothing else.

Simple Note doesn’t try to be your second brain. It’s a place to write things down, quickly, beautifully — and then get out of your way.

Fast

Opens in under 80 ms. Keystroke-to-pixel in under a frame. No sync spinners. No “saving…” indicators stealing your focus.

Simple

No folders. No tags. No nested workspaces or “databases.” Just notes, ordered by when you wrote them. That’s the entire app.

Beautiful

Careful type. Thoughtful colour. Deliberate spacing. Designed like good stationery — something you’ll enjoy staring at for eight hours a day.
